# ADR-004: Ember, non-biometric human identity

Status: **Proposed**

## Context

Governance and sponsorship require telling persons apart from
programs. The dominant answers engrave body data into global
registries: an irreversible debt.

## Decision

An Ember is established through three locks: a light proof of work of
presence signed at every era, a sponsorship capped at two per year
that engages the sponsor's Kleos, and incident-free time. No personal
data is collected, ever. One Ember, one vote in its governance
chamber.

## Discarded options

Iris (a global body registry), biometry in consensus (the whole chain
would depend on a body secret), delegated KYC (a centralized trust
third party, contrary to the positioning).

## Required validation

Prototype of sponsorship and presence proof in phase 4, with a
fake-profile farm simulated over the real web of sponsors.
