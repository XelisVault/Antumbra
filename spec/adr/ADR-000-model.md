# ADR-000: the architecture decision model

Status: **Accepted** (process)

## Context

Every structural choice in ANTUMBRA commits the network for years and
can only be corrected through a fork. Decisions must therefore be
written, argued, dated and numbered before a single line of code,
following the roadmap of the whitepaper (phase 1).

## Format

Each ADR contains: a status (Proposed, Accepted, Superseded,
Rejected), the context, the decision, the discarded options and their
motives, the consequences, and the validation criterion (prototype,
simulation or audit) required before final acceptance.

The ADRs are written in English, without em dashes, in the same style
as the whitepaper: they are specification documents.
