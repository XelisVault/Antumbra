\section{Thymus: Immunity as a Market}\label{sec:thymus}

More important than any governance layer is this one, because
the models of the next years arrive as attackers first. A chain
that waits to be attacked elsewhere has already lost the window;
a chain that pays for its own breakage, automatically, on proof,
turns the most capable attackers of the era into its most
productive sentinels. Thymus is that organ: always on, never
negotiating, structurally unable to stop the money. The honest
claim it supports is not ``without a fault'' but three published
numbers, mean time to detect, to contain, to repair, measured on
chain. Relatively very resistant means this market exists and
stays solvent; it does not mean the network is perfect, and
nothing in this paper is allowed to mean that.

\subsection{The nine invariants}

Every node replays them, every Sentinel replays them, and each is
a test in the Arena. The count is frozen at genesis: an invariant
that stops being checkable is a rail C change with a full
proposal behind it, never a silent edit.

\subsection{Canaries and alerts}

Canaries are synthetic transactions with known results, run on
isolated test accounts even on mainnet: a balance delta, a
nullifier count, a fee total, exact-match checked, every
deviation an alert. The alert machine is a fixed state transition,
implemented in the \texttt{antumbra-immune} crate and
cross-validated vector by vector: an alert is recorded, counted
in the open-critical metric, and paid if a replayable proof
follows. Freezing has exactly one trigger and two sources.

\paragraph{NO-GO, constitutional.} Thymus freezes an upgrade,
never a payment. The type of an alert target has no payment
variant: the freeze surface of the protocol cannot express
``stop the money'', and adding such a variant is a rail D change
that rewrites this section. A lying Sentinel is a nuisance, not a
halt: without a proof it needs two honest colleagues to agree
within the window, and a Sentinel that raises false alerts to
freeze a rival's upgrade loses its retainer and its bond, because
the false alert is itself a finding of the invariant that did not
break. A single lying proof is impossible by construction: the
proof replays or it does not.

\subsection{The bounty: a market, not a committee}

An alert with a replayable proof (script plus vector) pays
automatically from the immunity pole, forty percent of the
treasury budget while the treasury lives. The payout is
$250\,\mathrm{ATU} \times \sigma \times u$ where $\sigma$ is the
severity factor (critical 1.0, high 0.5, medium 0.25, low 0.1)
and $u$ the uniqueness: the first report of a proof hash takes
the full factor, later reports a quarter, documentation price.
No committee sits in the loop, no discretion exists to capture,
and the market becomes more liquid exactly as attacking models
become more capable: that is the entire thesis, and the reason
the pole is the largest one. The funding question after the
treasury sunset is answered where it belongs: a constitutional
vote of the three chambers recharges the pole, or the bounties
become private jobs, the v1.2 line, unchanged.

\subsection{The honest metrics: MTTD, MTTC, MTTR}

The word ``perfect'' is banned from this paper, its README and
its marketing; what replaces it is measured. Mean time to detect
(the height gap between an injected fault and the first alert),
to contain (fault to freeze), and to repair (freeze to resolve)
are on-chain state the machine computes, not adjectives a
marketing page claims. The phase table sets the targets; a
scene that cannot measure its target does not light the next
one. NO-GO, published with the numbers.

\subsection{What remains, named}

The residual risk of an immunity market is threefold and
stated here rather than in a footnote. A state actor that
outbids the bounty pole can lengthen the window; the defense is
the pole's size and the diversity of the attacker pool, and the
residual is real, as in every public chain. A Thymus that stops
finding is a culture dying, not a system failing: Thymus is not
a rail anyone may switch off, and its budget is the last line of
the treasury table. And the metrics themselves are gameable at
the margin by disclosure timing; the contest window and the
uniqueness factor price that game. ``Relatively very
resistant'' is the strongest honest sentence in this section,
and it is the one the whole layer is built to earn.
